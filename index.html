2026 First-person adventure Released 1.44 MB cap

Floppy Dungeon

C++17Direct3D 11No engineText-mode rendererProcedural

Solo, contest entry

A first-person adventure on a procedurally generated island, with melee combat, parries and dodges, a bow and a handful of abilities. I made it for 2P Game Arcade's 1.44MB Game Development Contest, where the whole game had to fit on a single floppy disk, and it won the NEXT STAGE Special Award.

It ships as one exe under 1,474,560 bytes with nothing to install, written in C++ on Direct3D 11 without an engine or any art files. The island, the buildings and the music are all generated when you press start. Everything on screen is text. The renderer picks a character for each cell instead of running an ASCII filter over a normal 3D image.

2026 VR Stealth Released on itch.io

Zero Day: Aftermath

VRStealthUnreal Engine 5C++OpenXR

Programmer at Negentropy Studio, final-year project, team of 3

A VR stealth game where you play a damaged maintenance droid, searching a ruined Earth for the last human survivors while the machines that wiped them out hunt you. I wrote the gameplay code, including the enemy AI. Enemies work as a squad. They see and hear you (walls block both), react when they find a body and try to flank you.

Most interactions are physical. You reload by pushing magazines in with your hand, and you heal by drinking fuel, which runs on a liquid simulation. To hold the frame rate VR needs, I built a LOD and hibernation system that puts distant actors to sleep.

2025 Roguelite FPS Released

Into the Dark

FPSSingle-player PVEUnreal Engine 5Cel-shaded 3D

Programmer at Outsiders Studios, team of 3

A cel-shaded roguelite shooter. You play a knight with a crossbow in one hand and a shield in the other, fighting through goblin caves to rescue the princess. I programmed the procedural map generation and the wave spawner, where every room runs its own waves and the difficulty goes up each level. I also did the shield blocking, timed off animation notifies, and the health drops.

Co-op mod In development Release v0.1.4

GLTPS1 Co-op

GLTPS1 is a PS1-style Girls' Last Tour fan game that was left unfinished. I added online co-op to it: one player is Chito, the other is Yuuri, and they travel the same world together. Both girls, the Kettenkrad, gunfire and the NPCs stay in sync, and the connection goes direct over UDP, falling back to Epic's relay when a router gets in the way.

Now I'm rebuilding a lot of its systems, starting with an open world that both players generate from the same seed.
