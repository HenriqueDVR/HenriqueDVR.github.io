Zero Day: Aftermath

Role: Programmer · final-year project · team of 3

A VR stealth game where you play a defective maintenance droid searching a half-ruined Earth for the last human survivors, hunted by the machines that wiped them out. I wrote the whole gameplay codebase in C++ on Unreal Engine 5 and OpenXR. Squad-coordinated enemy AI with sight and hearing occlusion, corpse detection and flanking. Your hands are physical and can interact with the environment, magazines are loaded by hand, and healing means actually drinking fuel, complete with a little liquid emulation. A custom LOD and hibernation system keeps all of it running at VR frame rate.

Into the Dark

Role: Programmer · team of 3

A cel-shaded FPS where a knight fights through goblin-infested caves to rescue the princess with just a crossbow in one hand, shield in the other. I built the procedural map generation, the per-room wave spawn system with per-level difficulty scaling, shield blocking driven by animation notifies, and the health drop system.
